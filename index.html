1. Formal Letter Format

औपचारिक पत्र — principal, editor या किसी अधिकारी को लिखा जाता है।

Sender's Address Date Receiver's Designation Receiver's Address Subject: __________________ Respected Sir/Madam, [Write the purpose of your letter in clear paragraphs.] Thank you. Yours faithfully, Your Name

Example subject

Subject: Request for two days' leave

Tip: Keep the tone polite, use short paragraphs and do not use casual language.

2. Informal Letter Format

अनौपचारिक पत्र — friends और family के लिए।

Your Address Date Dear [Name], How are you? I hope you are doing well. [Write your news, feelings or invitation in friendly language.] Give my regards to everyone. Yours lovingly, Your Name

3. Notice Writing Format

Notice कम शब्दों में जरूरी जानकारी देता है।

NAME OF SCHOOL / ORGANISATION NOTICE Date: __________ TITLE / HEADING This is to inform all students that __________________. Date/Time: __________ Venue: ______________ Interested students should __________________. Name Designation

Remember: Include what, when, where and who. Follow the word limit given in your question.

4. Message Writing Format

किसी अनुपस्थित व्यक्ति के लिए छोटा संदेश।

MESSAGE Date: _______ Time: _______ Dear [Name], [Person who called] called to say that __________________. Please __________________. [Your Name]

Tip: Do not add unnecessary details. Include the caller, main information and action required.

5. Application Writing Format

School application का सामान्य format।

To, The Principal, [School Name] [City] Date: __________ Subject: Application for leave Respected Sir/Madam, I am a student of Class ____ Section ____. I am unable to attend school because __________________. Kindly grant me leave for ____ days, from ____ to ____. Thank you. Yours obediently, Name: __________ Class/Section: __________ Roll No.: __________

6. Paragraph & Essay Writing

Easy structure

  1. Introduction: Introduce the topic.
  2. Main body: Explain 2–4 important points with examples.
  3. Conclusion: End with your opinion or a short summary.
Opening example — Importance of Education:
Education is one of the most important parts of our life. It gives us knowledge, confidence and the ability to make better decisions.

📚 English Grammar

Simple definitions, Hindi meaning and examples.

1. Noun (संज्ञा)

A noun is the name of a person, place, animal, thing or idea.

Examples: Riya, Delhi, dog, book, honesty.

Types: Proper, Common, Collective, Material and Abstract Nouns.

2. Pronoun (सर्वनाम)

A pronoun is a word used instead of a noun.

Riya is kind. She helps everyone.
Examples: I, we, you, he, she, it, they, who.

Types: Personal, Possessive, Reflexive, Demonstrative, Relative and Interrogative.

3. Verb (क्रिया)

A verb tells us about an action or state.

Examples: run, eat, write, is, are, seem.
She reads every day.

Verbs can be main/helping, action/linking, and regular/irregular.

4. Adverb (क्रिया-विशेषण)

An adverb adds information about a verb, adjective or another adverb. It can tell how, when, where or how often.

He runs quickly. She arrived yesterday. They are very happy.

5. Adjective (विशेषण)

An adjective describes a noun or pronoun.

A beautiful flower. Three small birds.

6. Preposition (संबंध सूचक शब्द)

A preposition shows the relationship of a noun/pronoun to another word.

The book is on the table. We meet at 5 pm. She lives in India.

Common prepositions: in, on, at, under, over, between, among, behind, beside, since, for, from, to.

7. Conjunction (समुच्चयबोधक)

A conjunction joins words or sentences.

I was tired, but I finished my work. Riya and Siya are friends.

8. Interjection (विस्मयादिबोधक)

An interjection expresses a sudden feeling.

Wow! That is amazing. Oh! I forgot my book.

9. Subject–Verb Agreement

The verb must agree with its subject in number and person.

SubjectCorrect verbExample
He / She / Itis / has / playsShe plays cricket.
Iam / have / playI am ready.
You / We / Theyare / have / playThey are happy.
Remember: “He go to school” is incorrect. Say “He goes to school.”

10. Tenses (काल)

Tenses show when an action happens: present, past or future.

TenseExample
Simple PresentI play.
Present ContinuousI am playing.
Present PerfectI have played.
Simple PastI played.
Past ContinuousI was playing.
Simple FutureI will play.

11. Articles (A, An, The)

A comes before a consonant sound; an before a vowel sound; the for something specific.

a book • an apple • an hour • the Sun

12. Active & Passive Voice

Active voice focuses on the doer. Passive voice focuses on the action or receiver.

Active: The boy kicked the ball.
Passive: The ball was kicked by the boy.

13. Direct & Indirect Speech

Direct speech gives the exact words. Indirect speech reports what someone said.

Direct: He said, “I am happy.”
Indirect: He said that he was happy.

14. Modals

Modals are helping verbs that express ability, permission, advice or possibility.

can (ability), may (permission/possibility), should (advice), must (necessity), might (possibility).

15. Punctuation

Punctuation marks make writing clear.

Full stop (.) • Comma (,) • Question mark (?) • Exclamation mark (!) • Apostrophe (')

Example: “Where are you going?” asked Rohan.

16. Singular & Plural

Singular means one; plural means more than one.

book → books • city → cities • child → children • mouse → mice

17. Degrees of Comparison

Adjectives can show positive, comparative and superlative degrees.

tall → taller → tallest
good → better → best

18. Gerund & Infinitive

A gerund is an -ing verb form used as a noun. An infinitive is usually “to + base verb”.

Swimming is fun. / I want to learn.
